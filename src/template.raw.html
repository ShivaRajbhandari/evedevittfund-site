Ways we help

Three funds. One promise.

We are here so Idaho’s trans and non-binary youth can get care, get to college and keep organizing.

Travel Fund

Need to travel for care?

We help pay for gas, airfare, lodging, childcare and food ($70 a day) for Idaho trans and non-binary kids and their families traveling out of state for gender-affirming care. Need-based and confidential.

Travel Fund

How it works

  1. Tell us about your trip

    A short form, about 5 minutes: where you live, where you’re going, when, and what it will cost. If we approve your request, a second private form asks for a photo ID and your doctor’s name, so we can book and pay for your trip.

  2. Quick, kind check-in

    Two board members reach out by the way you told us is safe (text, call or email) for a short conversation. It’s how we make sure every dollar reaches a kid who needs it.

  3. We pay for the trip

    Where we can, we book or pay flights and lodging directly. Gas, childcare and per diem are reimbursed with receipts. You don’t need to front the money.

Your privacy matters. only the board members reviewing your request see your information, we ask for the minimum we need, and we never share, sell or publish it.

About

Named for Eve Devitt

In 2023, after Idaho banned gender-affirming care for minors, five Idaho students started a fund so kids could still get the care they need. It carries the name of Eve Devitt, an Idaho human rights activist who refuses to back down in the fight for trans visibility, inclusion and care.

We are proud to stand with Idaho’s trans and non-binary youth. Loudly, and for the long haul.
